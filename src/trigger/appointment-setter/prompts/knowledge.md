## Who are Northbeam Advisory?
Northbeam Advisory is a Nairobi-based consultancy that helps owner-run service businesses (clinics, salons, agencies, trades, studios, schools) fix their operations and grow profitably. We focus on three things: pricing, sales process, and automating busywork. Founded in 2019 by Achieng Odhiambo. We work with clients across Kenya and East Africa, and remotely with clients worldwide.

## What services do you offer and how much do they cost?
- **Discovery Call**: free, 30 minutes. We learn about your business and tell you honestly whether we can help.
- **Strategy Session**: KES 30,000 (or USD 230 for clients outside Kenya), 30 minutes. A focused deep-dive on one problem (pricing, sales pipeline, or automation), and you leave with a written action plan.
- **Growth Retainer**: from KES 180,000 per month (or from USD 1,400 per month). Ongoing advisory with two calls a month, WhatsApp and email support, and hands-on help implementing the changes. Minimum 3 months.
Exact retainer pricing depends on the size of the business and what's needed, and is agreed after a Discovery Call.

## How do calls work?
Every call is a 30-minute Google Meet video call. Once booked, we send you the Google Meet link right here on WhatsApp. No software to install, just tap the link at the time. Calls are in English; our consultants also speak Swahili and are happy to mix both.

## What are your opening hours?
Calls run Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM {{TZ}} (East Africa Time, Nairobi). The last slot starts at 4:30 PM {{TZ}}. We're closed on weekends and Kenyan public holidays. You can book up to 14 days ahead, with at least 2 hours' notice. Clients in other countries are welcome; we'll show you times in your own timezone if you ask.

## Where are you located? Can I visit your office?
Our office is in Westlands, Nairobi (Westlands Business Park). All Discovery Calls and Strategy Sessions happen on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere. Retainer clients in Nairobi can arrange in-person working sessions at our office or at their business once they've signed up.

## How do I pay?
The Discovery Call is free. Strategy Sessions are paid before the call: after you book, the team sends you an invoice here on WhatsApp with the M-Pesa Paybill number, card link and bank details (during business hours). Ava doesn't have payment numbers herself. International clients pay the USD price; if you pay by card in GBP or EUR, your bank converts it at the day's rate (roughly GBP 180 or EUR 215 for a Strategy Session). Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance and can be paid by M-Pesa, bank transfer or card.

## What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
You can reschedule or cancel for free up to 24 hours before your call. Inside 24 hours, paid Strategy Sessions are non-refundable but can be moved once. Just message us here on WhatsApp to change anything.

## Do you offer refunds or guarantees?
If you're not happy with a Strategy Session, tell us within 7 days and we'll refund it in full. Retainers can be cancelled with 30 days' notice after the 3-month minimum. We don't promise specific results, because every business is different, but we agree clear goals with you up front and review progress every month on retainers.

## Who will I speak to?
Discovery Calls and Strategy Sessions are with Achieng Odhiambo (founder) or Daniel Mutua (senior consultant). Both have 10+ years running and advising service businesses in East Africa. We don't share consultants' personal phone numbers or emails; all contact goes through this WhatsApp line, and the team replies here.

## Do you work with my type of business?
We work best with owner-led service businesses with roughly KES 10 million to KES 500 million in yearly revenue (about USD 75k to 4M), and usually 3 to 100 staff. Typical clients: clinics and dental practices, salons and spas, agencies, schools, logistics and trades businesses. We don't work with e-commerce product brands, startups that haven't launched yet, or large corporates.

## Do you have case studies or references?
Yes. We walk through relevant case studies on the Discovery Call, matched to your industry (for example healthcare, beauty or professional services). With our clients' permission, we can connect serious prospects with a reference client after the Discovery Call.

## How do you handle confidential or patient data?
We follow Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019). We're happy to sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive. We never need patients' or customers' personal records to do our work; we work from business numbers, processes and anonymised data.

## What are the contract terms?
Discovery Calls and Strategy Sessions are one-off, with no contract. Growth Retainers have a simple agreement: a 3-month minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice to cancel.

## How quickly does the team reply?
Our team replies on this WhatsApp line Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM {{TZ}}, usually within an hour. Messages sent outside those hours are answered first thing the next business morning.
